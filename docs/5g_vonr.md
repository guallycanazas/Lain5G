# 5G VoNR

The `5g-vonr-sim` software scenario integrates 5G SA, two DNNs, and a lab IMS.
Its classification is maintained in the
[canonical capability table](../README.md#canonical-capability-status).

A local run, `run-20260725-213427`, was reported with 25/25 `PASS` checks, but
its artifact is not published in this repository and cannot be independently
reviewed.

The [public attempt `run-20260723-055328`](../results/public/5g-vonr-sim/run-20260723-055328.json)
is the only available public evidence and remains `BLOCKED` and `NOT_VALIDATED`.
The scenario is also not part of the current public operational catalog. RF,
commercial UEs, audio, and RTP performance remain outside the publicly
validated scope.
